## What we learned (shareable)

- Funded translational projects follow a visible formula: quantified burden →
  named platform + one differentiation sentence → numeric TRL journey →
  safety/privacy positioning → commercial artifacts (spin-out name, pilot
  contract) as deliverables.
- Anchoring a health-AI product to an existing reimbursed care workflow (rather
  than inventing new clinician behaviour) massively strengthens both the
  adoption story and the evaluation design.
- Keeping personal data out of the core knowledge layer (guidelines-only KG,
  transient patient context) turns GDPR from a risk section into a design
  feature.
